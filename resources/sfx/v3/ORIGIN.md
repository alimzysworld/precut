# v3 sounds

Made for the precut launch video (26 Oct 2026, sidething-launchpad-site `remotion/public/precut/sfx/v3-*.wav`) and copied here unchanged for precut's sound library (ONB2, 29 Oct).

- Synthesised by Claude: boom, clean, click, keys, land, launch, riser, rush, shutter, slam, subdrop, thud, tick, timestop, turn, whip.
- Processed from precut's own CC0 v2 sounds (src/main/sfx.ts): hit, impact, impact-dark, pop, swish, whoosh.

Every file is peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, 48 kHz stereo, as it was. No third-party audio is in them. They ship with precut under its own terms, with nothing owed by the person who uses them.
