# Sound effect licences

Sounds shipped inside precut in `sfx/` (source: `resources/sfx/`), listed in `manifest.json`. Checked 2026-10-31.

Every sound here is either dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 or made for precut. None needs attribution; the people and packs below are credited anyway, as Kenney asks and as the Freesound FAQ's "you can't claim you are the author" implies. Nothing is owed by a person who uses these sounds in their own videos.

Built by `scripts/sfx-synth/build-library.mjs` (process, level, encode, measure) and `scripts/sfx-synth/synth3.py` (the sounds made in code). "Level" is the gain applied before encoding (role levels in the manifest note).

## Made for precut

Ours, shipped with precut under its own terms; nothing is owed by the person who uses them.

- `synth/`: made in code by `scripts/sfx-synth/synth3.py` (pure Python, seeded, so they rebuild the same): long slide, short riser, tonal riser, reverse swell, downer, bass drop, bubble, glitch burst, light-leak shimmer. Then trimmed, faded, levelled and encoded like the rest.
- `v3/`: the 22 launch-video sounds (origin in `v3/ORIGIN.md`: synthesised by Claude, or processed from precut's own synth sounds). Re-encoded from WAV to 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis; the 20 in the browser were also tail-trimmed and levelled by role, the 2 retired ones (impact, launch) keep their level.
